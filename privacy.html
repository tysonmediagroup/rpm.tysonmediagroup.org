Privacy Notice

Effective Date: August 2026 • Registered Domain: rpm.tysonmediagroup.org

1. Overview & Scope

TYSON Media Group ("TYSON Media Group", "we", "us", or "our") operates rpm.tysonmediagroup.org and associated media broadcast properties. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data integrity, confidentiality, and respect for user privacy across our web platform and digital dispatches.

2. Information Collection

We collect minimal personal data strictly necessary to deliver automotive journalism, broadcast video content, and process production inquiries:

  • Inquiry Submissions: When you submit a press, vehicle loan, or production inquiry via our contact portals, we collect your name, email address, corporate affiliation, and project message.
  • Embedded Content & Platform Telemetry: When watching embedded video streams powered by YouTube, YouTube’s standard privacy policy and cookie framework apply.
  • Technical Logging: Standard web server access logs (IP addresses, user-agent strings, and timestamp data) are maintained solely for security audit and performance diagnostics.

3. Purpose of Processing

Information gathered through our portals is utilized exclusively for:

  • Direct communication regarding media commissions, vehicle testing logistics, and editorial dispatches.
  • Ensuring website availability, server defense, and fast multimedia streaming.
  • Fulfilling corporate obligations and legal compliance under applicable media regulations.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Our primary domain does not employ invasive tracking cookies or third-party behavioral advertising scripts. Third-party video embeds (such as YouTube Theater integration) may store essential session tokens governed by Google's Privacy Policies.

5. Data Disclosure & Sharing

TYSON Media Group does not sell, rent, monetize, or lease your personal information to third parties. Data is only disclosed when required by law or to protect the safety, rights, and intellectual property of TYSON Media Group.
